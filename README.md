ChargeQ — EV Charger Waitlist App

A web app I designed and built to solve a real pain point at our office: queuing up for the limited number of EV charging spaces in the parking lot

**Built with:** Replit

---

## The Problem
We only have 2 electric vehicle chargers at the office but there are probably about a dozen employees who have EVs who like to charge while at the office.  It's been an early bird gets the worm system, but then people who really need to charge have to watch the spots to see if anyone moves.


## The Solution
ChargeQ is a web app that lets users check into the EV charging spots or join the waitlist.  When a user checks out, the next person on the waitlist is notified so they can take the spot.

## Product Decisions Worth Noting
I went with a FIFO queue, manual check in / check out, and limiting usage to users with my company email domain.
